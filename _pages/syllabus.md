objectives:
- objective: "Connect computational approaches with insights from philosophy, cognitive science, data ethics, and the social sciences to deepen understanding of intelligence as both an artificial and human phenomenon."
- objective: "Present technical findings and ethical analyses clearly in written, oral, and visual forms appropriate to academic and professional audiences."
- objective: "Explain the mechanics and purposes of heuristic search algorithms such as A*, minimax, and genetic algorithms."
- objective: "Describe the differences between knowledge-based reasoning systems and data-driven learning systems."

goals:
- goal: "synthesize the core principles of artificial intelligence, including knowledge representation, reasoning, learning, and decision-making."
- goal: "Employ AI algorithms and frameworks to design, implement, and evaluate intelligent systems in diverse domains."
- goal: "Articulate and apply ethical frameworks in the design, deployment, and governance of AI technologies, emphasizing fairness, transparency, accountability, and human well-being."
